Our Mission

"Too many veterans are suffering in silence. Too many are self-medicating without guidance, without information, without the safety net of professional support. They deserve better."

Engaged Frequency Lab (EFL)™ was founded by a United States Army Captain who understands the weight of service — and the silence that follows it. After two decades of military service and a personal journey through the VA system, the founder recognized a critical gap: veterans needed access to credible, well-researched information about the full spectrum of healing options available to them — not just what fits inside a prescription bottle.

EFL exists to fill that gap. We are an education-first platform — we don't sell supplements, we don't replace doctors, and we don't promise miracles. What we offer is something many veterans have never received: honest, science-backed information about emerging therapies, plant-based medicine, holistic practices, and the legal landscape surrounding them. We believe informed veterans make empowered decisions about their own healing.

The Four Pillars

EFL operates across four integrated domains to serve the veteran community

Education

Research-driven content on psilocybin-assisted therapy, medical cannabis, kratom, terpene science, and traditional herbalism. Our flagship publication, Roots & Remedies, is a comprehensive guide to holistic healing for veterans in the Tallahassee region and beyond.

Consulting

One-on-one and group guidance for veterans navigating the complex landscape of alternative therapies. We help you understand your options, find qualified providers, access clinical trials, and make informed decisions about your wellness journey.

Advocacy

Policy education and veteran rights awareness. We track legislation affecting veteran access to cannabis, psychedelics, and holistic therapies — including VHA Directive 1315, the June 2025 House amendment, and state-level regulatory changes.

Media & Content

Ebooks, presentations, resource guides, and community outreach materials — all free, all evidence-based, all designed for veterans and the people who support them. Every piece of content is an original EFL™ production.

The Science

Evidence-based research is reshaping how we understand veteran healing

Psilocybin-Assisted Therapy

Psilocybin activates serotonin 5-HT2A receptors and disrupts the Default Mode Network — breaking the rigid thought loops of PTSD. The VA is conducting clinical trials at 9 medical centers. The DEA requested 30,000 grams for research in 2026.

Breakthrough Therapy

Medical Cannabis & the ECS

Your body has a built-in cannabinoid system that regulates mood, pain, sleep, and fear extinction. CB1 receptors in the amygdala play a direct role in how traumatic memories lose their hold. PTSD is a qualifying condition in Florida.

38+ States Legal

Kratom & Opioid Alternatives

Kratom's primary alkaloid mitragynine activates opioid pain pathways WITHOUT recruiting the beta-arrestin-2 signaling that causes respiratory depression and overdose. NIDA is conducting its first-ever clinical trial of purified mitragynine.
